## How I approach the work

When a service goes down, I first try to understand who is affected and what the impact is. Then I check what changed, look at the available logs or monitoring, and narrow the issue down before making changes. For planned work, I want a baseline, a clear maintenance window and a way back if the change does not go as expected.

I have learned that fixing the technical issue is only part of the job. People need updates they can understand. The next engineer needs a handover that is worth reading. If I make a mistake, I should own my part, explain what happened and help put it right. I am still learning, and I am comfortable checking documentation or asking someone with more experience rather than pretending to know an answer.

For more detail, see [my operating principles](OPERATING-PRINCIPLES.md).
